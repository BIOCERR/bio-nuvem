# Overview

BIO – Regenerative AI Platform is committed to protecting user privacy and promoting responsible data practices.

The public version of BIO has been designed according to the principle of Privacy by Design, minimizing the collection and processing of personally identifiable information (PII).

---

# Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to:

- Public website
- Public GitHub repositories
- Public documentation
- Demonstration environments
- Educational materials

It does not apply to future commercial deployments or private enterprise implementations, which may have additional privacy requirements.

---

# Data Collection

The public BIO platform is designed to operate with minimal collection of personal information.

The platform primarily uses:

- Public documentation
- Demonstration content
- Simulated datasets
- Non-personally identifiable operational information

---

# Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

The public platform does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information unless voluntarily provided by users through external communication channels.

Examples of PII include:

- Name
- Email address
- Telephone number
- Physical address
- Government identification
- Biometric information

Public demonstrations do not require this information.

---

# Non-Personal Data

The platform may process anonymous or non-personally identifiable information such as:

- Sustainability indicators
- Demonstration datasets
- Digital Twin simulations
- System metrics
- Public educational examples

These data cannot be used to identify an individual.

---

# Cookies

The public website itself does not intentionally use cookies for user profiling.

However, third-party hosting providers or services may use essential technical cookies necessary for website operation.

Users should also consult the privacy policies of those third-party providers.

---

# Third-Party Services

The public platform may reference or be hosted through services such as:

- GitHub
- Vercel

These services maintain their own privacy policies and terms of service.

BIOCERR is not responsible for third-party privacy practices.

# Applicable Regulations

The public BIO platform has been designed considering internationally recognized privacy principles, including:

- Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD - Brazil)

# Children's Privacy

The public platform is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

# Data Retention

The public platform does not intentionally retain personally identifiable information collected through its demonstration environment.

If personal information is voluntarily provided through external communication channels, it will be retained only as necessary for the intended purpose and in accordance with applicable law.

# International Transfers

Public repositories and websites may be hosted by international cloud service providers.
