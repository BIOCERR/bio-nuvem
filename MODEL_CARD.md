# Overview

BIO is the Regenerative Artificial Intelligence Platform developed by BIOCERR.

The platform was designed to support decentralized industrial ecosystems based on Industry 5.0, Circular Economy principles and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Rather than acting as a general-purpose AI assistant, BIO is intended to provide intelligent support for regenerative manufacturing, digital infrastructure and sustainable industrial decision-making.

---

# Purpose

BIO aims to:

- Support decentralized manufacturing networks (MDPS).
- Improve industrial resilience.
- Promote circular economy principles.
- Optimize resource utilization.
- Reduce environmental impacts.
- Assist decision making using regenerative intelligence.
- Integrate digital infrastructure into industrial ecosystems.

---

# Intended Use

BIO is intended for:

- Industrial ecosystems
- Circular manufacturing
- Smart factories
- Industry 5.0 applications
- Digital Twins
- IoT environments
- Sustainability management
- ESG monitoring
- Decision support
- Educational demonstrations
- Innovation projects

---

# Out of Scope

BIO is not intended to:

- Replace human decision makers.
- Make autonomous legal decisions.
- Replace engineering validation.
- Replace environmental licensing.
- Replace human ethical judgment.

Final decisions should always remain under human responsibility.
