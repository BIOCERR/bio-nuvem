# Overview

This document describes the publicly available components, data usage principles and transparency practices of the BIO – Regenerative AI Platform.

BIO is a Regenerative Artificial Intelligence platform developed by BIOCERR to support sustainable industrial ecosystems, Industry 5.0, decentralized manufacturing and Circular Economy initiatives.

This datasheet refers only to the public version of the platform.

---

# Purpose

BIO is designed to:

- Support regenerative industrial ecosystems.
- Assist decision-making.
- Improve sustainability indicators.
- Integrate digital infrastructure.
- Support Industry 5.0 concepts.
- Promote ethical Artificial Intelligence.

---

# Intended Users

The public platform is intended for:

- Researchers
- Universities
- Innovation ecosystems
- Public institutions
- Companies
- Cities
- Sustainability professionals
- Technology partners

---

# Public Data

The public platform may use:

- Demonstration datasets
- Public sustainability indicators
- Educational examples
- System simulations
- Non-personal operational information

The public platform does not require personally identifiable information (PII) for demonstration purposes.

---

# Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

The public repository is designed to minimize or avoid the collection of personally identifiable information.

Where personal information is required by external services, those services remain responsible for their own privacy policies.

---

# Data Sources

Public demonstrations may include:

- Simulated datasets
- Public sustainability references
- Public SDG information
- Educational examples
- User-generated demonstration inputs

No confidential industrial datasets are included in this public repository.

---

# Training Data

The public BIO repository does not distribute the proprietary datasets, internal knowledge bases or confidential information used in private development activities.

Protected datasets remain under the ownership of BIOCERR and are intentionally excluded from the public repository.

---

# Model Information

BIO is presented publicly as a Regenerative Artificial Intelligence platform.

The public repository documents the platform architecture, governance and intended use.

Details regarding proprietary AI implementation, internal algorithms and protected industrial logic are intentionally omitted.

---

# Evaluation

Public demonstrations are intended for educational and informational purposes.

Performance evaluations of proprietary AI components are not included in this repository.

---

# Limitations

The public platform:

- Does not expose proprietary algorithms.
- Does not expose confidential industrial processes.
- Does not include production datasets.
- Does not represent a complete production deployment.

It is intended to document the public architecture and vision of the BIO ecosystem.

---

# Ethical Considerations

BIO follows principles of:

- Responsible AI
- Transparency
- Sustainability
- Human-centered AI
- Environmental responsibility
- Ethical innovation

The platform is designed to support human decision-making rather than replace human responsibility.
