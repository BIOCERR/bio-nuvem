# 🌍 BIO – Regenerative AI Platform
## Community Guidelines

Version: 1.0  
Last Updated: July 2026

---

# Welcome

Thank you for your interest in the BIO – Regenerative AI Platform.

BIO is an open collaborative initiative that promotes responsible Artificial Intelligence, Industry 5.0, sustainability and regenerative innovation.

We welcome researchers, developers, educators, organizations and contributors who share these values.

---

# Our Community

We strive to build a community that is:

- Respectful
- Inclusive
- Collaborative
- Ethical
- Transparent
- Scientifically responsible
- Environmentally responsible

Everyone should feel welcome to participate.

---

# Collaboration Principles

We encourage contributors to:

- Share knowledge openly.
- Communicate respectfully.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Respect different perspectives.
- Support collaborative learning.
- Contribute responsibly.

---

# Respect

Treat every community member with dignity and professionalism.

Respect differences in:

- Culture
- Language
- Gender
- Nationality
- Background
- Professional experience
- Opinions

---

# Communication

Healthy discussions are encouraged.

When communicating:

- Focus on ideas rather than individuals.
- Be constructive.
- Remain courteous.
- Ask questions respectfully.
- Support statements with evidence whenever possible.

---

# Unacceptable Behaviour

The following are not permitted:

- Harassment
- Bullying
- Hate speech
- Personal attacks
- Discrimination
- Threats
- Intimidation
- Deliberate misinformation intended to cause harm
- Illegal activities
- Offensive or abusive language

---

# Safe Environment

BIO seeks to provide a professional environment where contributors can collaborate safely.

The current public platform does not provide:

- Anonymous public discussions
- Open chat rooms
- Social networking features
- Unrestricted user-generated content

Community interaction primarily occurs through controlled collaboration channels such as GitHub.

---

# Young Users

The public BIO platform is not specifically designed for children.

The project does not intentionally collect personal information from minors.

Future services intended for younger audiences will adopt additional safeguards where appropriate.

---

# Reporting Concerns

Community members are encouraged to report:

- Harassment
- Discrimination
- Offensive behaviour
- Security concerns
- Policy violations

Reports may be submitted through the project's official communication channels.

All reports will be reviewed by the project maintainers.

---

# Moderation

Project maintainers may:

- Request clarification
- Remove inappropriate content
- Close discussions
- Reject contributions
- Restrict participation when necessary

Moderation decisions seek to protect the community while maintaining fairness and transparency.

---

# Intellectual Property

Contributors should respect:

- Copyright
- Open-source licenses
- Patents
- Trademarks
- Confidential information

Protected technologies owned by BIOCERR are not included in the public repositories.

---

# Responsible AI

BIO promotes Artificial Intelligence that:

- Supports people.
- Encourages ethical decision-making.
- Promotes sustainability.
- Respects human dignity.
- Contributes to society.
