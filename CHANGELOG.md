## Versioning Policy

BIO follows semantic versioning principles whenever applicable.

Version format:

Major.Minor.Patch

Example:

1.0.0

- Major → Significant architectural changes.
- Minor → New features and documentation.
- Patch → Bug fixes and minor improvements.
