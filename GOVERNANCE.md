# Overview

BIO – Regenerative AI Platform is governed according to the principles of transparency, ethical responsibility, sustainability and continuous innovation.

The governance model balances open collaboration with the protection of proprietary technologies, ensuring that the public ecosystem can evolve while preserving the intellectual property associated with BIOCERR.

---

# Governance Principles

The BIO platform is guided by the following principles:

- Transparency
- Accountability
- Ethical Artificial Intelligence
- Sustainability
- Human-centered Innovation
- Industry 5.0
- Continuous Improvement
- Responsible Collaboration

# Decision Making

Strategic decisions are based on:

- Technical feasibility
- Ethical principles
- Sustainability
- Long-term public benefit
- Security
- Compliance with applicable laws

Major architectural changes are evaluated before implementation.

# Open Collaboration

BIO encourages collaboration on public components including:

- Documentation
- Educational resources
- Public website
- Demonstrations
- Examples
- Community improvements

Contributions are reviewed before acceptance.

---

# Intellectual Property

BIO follows a hybrid intellectual property model.

Open components include:

- Documentation
- Public website
- Educational resources
- Public demonstrations
- Public repositories

Protected components include:

- Patented technologies
- Proprietary industrial processes
- Confidential operational knowledge
- Industrial production methods
- Proprietary algorithms

These protected components remain under the ownership of BIOCERR.
