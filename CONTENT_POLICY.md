# 🌍 BIO – Regenerative AI Platform
## Content Policy

Version: 1.0  
Last Updated: July 2026

---

# Purpose

The BIO – Regenerative AI Platform is committed to providing educational, technical and institutional content that supports responsible innovation, sustainability and Industry 5.0.

This policy establishes the principles governing the publication, review and management of public content.

---

# Scope

This policy applies to:

- Public website
- GitHub repositories
- Documentation
- Images
- Videos
- Educational materials
- Demonstration content
- Public datasets
- Future public communication channels

---

# Content Principles

BIO publishes content that seeks to be:

- Accurate
- Educational
- Respectful
- Ethical
- Transparent
- Scientifically responsible
- Environmentally responsible

---

# Allowed Content

The platform may publish:

- Technical documentation
- Educational materials
- Sustainability information
- Industry 5.0 concepts
- Circular Economy content
- Artificial Intelligence documentation
- Public demonstrations
- Public images and graphics
- Public videos
- Software documentation
- Research dissemination
- Public project updates

---

# Prohibited Content

BIO does not intentionally publish or promote content involving:

- Illegal activities
- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
- Terrorist or extremist propaganda
- Hate speech
- Harassment
- Violence promotion
- Discrimination
- Fraud
- Malware
- Copyright infringement
- Personal attacks
- Misinformation intentionally created to cause harm

---

# User-Generated Content

The current public platform does not allow anonymous user-generated content or unrestricted public publishing.

Future interactive services may adopt additional moderation mechanisms if user-generated content becomes available.

---

# Content Review

Public content is reviewed by the BIOCERR project maintainers before publication whenever applicable.

The review process seeks to ensure:

- Technical accuracy
- Ethical consistency
- Appropriate language
- Compliance with applicable laws
- Respect for intellectual property

---

# Reporting Content

If inappropriate or illegal content is identified, users are encouraged to contact BIOCERR through the project's official communication channels.

Reported content will be reviewed by the project maintainers.

---

# Content Removal

BIOCERR reserves the right to:

- Correct content
- Update documentation
- Remove inappropriate material
- Remove obsolete information
- Remove content violating this policy

---

# Intellectual Property

Public documentation and open-source components are distributed according to their respective licenses.

Protected industrial knowledge, patented technologies and proprietary operational methods remain outside the public repositories.

---

# Responsible AI

BIO promotes the responsible use of Artificial Intelligence.

Content generated with AI assistance is subject to human review before public publication whenever appropriate.

---

# Transparency

The project seeks to maintain transparent public documentation regarding:

- Governance
- Security
- Privacy
- Responsible AI
- Sustainability
