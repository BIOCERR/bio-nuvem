# CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md

# 🌱 BIO – Regenerative AI Platform
## Code of Conduct

Version: 1.0  
Last Updated: July 2026

---

# Our Commitment

BIO – Regenerative AI Platform is committed to building an open, respectful, ethical and collaborative community.

We believe that technological innovation should strengthen cooperation, sustainability and human dignity while contributing to a regenerative future.

Every participant is expected to help create a welcoming environment for everyone.

---

# Our Values

The BIO community is guided by the following principles:

- Respect
- Collaboration
- Transparency
- Integrity
- Responsibility
- Sustainability
- Inclusion
- Continuous Learning
- Ethical Innovation

These principles apply to all interactions within the project.

---

# Expected Behavior

Community members are encouraged to:

- Treat everyone with respect.
- Share knowledge openly.
- Welcome constructive feedback.
- Encourage collaboration.
- Support diversity of perspectives.
- Respect different cultural backgrounds.
- Communicate professionally.
- Contribute responsibly.
- Promote sustainable innovation.

---

# Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks.
- Hate speech.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Deliberate misinformation.
- Publishing confidential information without authorization.
- Violating intellectual property rights.
- Disruptive or abusive behavior.

---

# Artificial Intelligence Ethics

BIO promotes responsible and ethical use of Artificial Intelligence.

Contributors are expected to:

- Prioritize human well-being.
- Respect privacy.
- Promote transparency.
- Avoid harmful applications.
- Consider environmental impacts.
- Encourage responsible innovation.

AI should support people—not replace human responsibility.

---

# Intellectual Property

This repository contains only public components of the BIO platform.

Protected technologies, patented inventions, proprietary operational logic and confidential industrial knowledge remain the intellectual property of BIOCERR.

Contributors must respect all applicable intellectual property rights.

---

# Sustainability

BIO encourages solutions that:

- Reduce waste.
- Improve resource efficiency.
- Strengthen local communities.
- Support Circular Economy.
- Contribute to the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

---

# Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Unacceptable behavior may result in:

- Private warning.
- Temporary restriction.
- Removal of contributions.
- Permanent exclusion from the project.

Responses will always seek fairness, proportionality and respect.
